# Privacy Policy — Tend

**Last updated: 6 September 2026**

Tend is designed to be private by default. It does not collect, monitor, or profile you. The information you create in Tend is unique to you and stays on your device, with optional synchronization through your Apple account.

---

## 1. Who we are

Tend is developed and published by Paul Edmondson-Jones. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@paul-ej.dev

---

## 2. Information Tend stores

Tend stores only the information you create or choose to keep in the app, along with the settings needed for the app to work. This information is used solely to provide Tend's features to you.

Your data is unique to you. Tend does not use it to build user profiles, infer interests or behaviour, make advertising audiences, or train models.

---

## 3. No collection, monitoring, or tracking

Tend does not collect personal information, usage information, device identifiers, diagnostics, or behavioural data.

Tend contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or profiling services. We do not operate servers that receive or store your Tend data.

---

## 4. iCloud and CloudKit sync

If iCloud is enabled for Tend, your app data is synchronized between your devices using Apple's CloudKit service and your private iCloud account. This lets your data remain available on your own devices.

We cannot access your private CloudKit data. Apple handles iCloud and CloudKit under its own terms and privacy policy. You can manage or disable iCloud sync in your device's iCloud settings.

---

## 5. Sharing and third parties

Tend does not sell, rent, share, or disclose your data to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The only external service used by Tend is Apple's iCloud/CloudKit service when you enable syncing. Your use of that service is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

---

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Your Tend data remains on your device and, if sync is enabled, in your personal iCloud account until you delete it. You can delete data from within Tend, remove the app from your device, and manage associated iCloud data through your device's iCloud storage settings.

Because we do not collect or retain a copy of your data, we cannot access, retrieve, or delete it for you.

---

## 7. Children

Tend does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Its data practices are the same for all users.

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Any future version will continue to describe how Tend handles your information.
